## Research brief

Beaver begins with the lived experience of people at home. The research sequence moves from routines and spatial constraints toward product opportunities; it does not begin by assuming a humanoid body or a predetermined feature list.

### Questions to investigate
- Which recurring home tasks create meaningful friction, and for whom?
- How do pets, visitors, and temporary residents change the way a home is used?
- Where do privacy, safety, trust, and control become non-negotiable?
- Which tasks require physical action, and which are better served by information or environmental integration?
- What capabilities are technically feasible at an acceptable level of reliability?

### Evidence standard
Every finding should carry its source, collection date, sample/context, confidence, and limitations. Until evidence is collected, statements remain hypotheses or research questions.
